# Chronology and evidence

This archive separates the period when a project was developed from the date on which its surviving snapshot was published. There was no `.git` or `.plastic` history in the ten supplied project folders. Original filesystem modification times and SHA-256 hashes were recorded before restoration; they support a chronology but cannot prove exact start dates or hours worked.

The author confirmed sole authorship and the learning / game-jam context on October 12, 2026. Most studies are connected with the author's Kodland Unity and Unity Pro learning period; the precise course assignment for each folder is not asserted.

## Other preserved folders

- **GameJamProjectN1:** one January 15, 2025 scene with four serialized GameObjects and no custom C# scripts. Preserved locally as a seed, not presented as a completed jam submission. This is distinct from the later Kenney Jam project in `SomeGame`.
- **The_Cursed_Prince_V1:** a January 21, 2024 empty scene and no custom C# scripts. The substantial combat prototype is `Cursed_Prince_Version_N1`.

## External date anchor

[Kenney Jam 2025](https://itch.io/jam/kenney-jam-2025) ran July 18–20, 2025, with the theme **POWER**. The author reports making `SomeGame` for this jam but missing the submission deadline following a power and connectivity outage during upload. The repository does not claim an official entry, rating or prize.

## What the dates do not mean

Imported asset dates are not project start dates. Folder creation / modification times after copying are not authorship evidence. Git commits made during restoration have their real current dates; old snapshots are not converted into a fabricated day-by-day development history.

The [source manifest](source-manifest.json) records the original path, preserved modification time, byte size and SHA-256 of every published source file. The complete private local audit also covers the imported assets.
